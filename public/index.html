Found something
worth adding?

Send a pull request. Include the original source and the words you'd like to post. A maintainer will check both before the fact becomes available to share.

  1. Find the original recordAn audit, a court filing, or published agency data is a good place to start.
  2. Say what it showsInclude the date and the relevant passage. Keep the claim as specific as the evidence allows.
  3. Send it for reviewAdd the record to the GitHub library and open a pull request. New facts never get added to anyone’s posting queue automatically.
Can this post to my account yet?

No. You can browse the library and try a schedule with sample text. The 100 drafts are available to read. Threads sign-in and posting stay off until maintainer review and platform setup are finished. The demo doesn't collect account details.

What happens when I disconnect?

Once the live service opens, disconnect will delete your saved connection, schedule, approvals, and activity. You'll also need to remove the app in Threads settings to revoke its permission there. A post already being sent can still appear.

What if a fact is wrong?

Open a GitHub issue with the fact ID and the evidence. Changing or withdrawing a fact stops queues that include it until their owners review it again. Anything already posted stays on Threads unless its owner changes it.
