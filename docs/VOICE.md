# Voice

Direct, conversational, specific. Write as someone explaining a small useful tool to a friend. The user asked to remove copy that sounds like AI or a marketing template.

Say what happens: pick posts, check sources, approve a schedule, pause, disconnect. Avoid slogans about trust, promises, empowerment or transparency. Keep dates, evidence qualifications and consent language precise. Don't turn an allegation into a finding to make the sentence punchier.

Use “posts,” “sources,” “schedule,” and “review.” Keep “immutable snapshots,” “bounded content library,” “cadence,” “gates,” and “simulation” in technical docs. Use contractions where they sound natural. Give errors a next step without manufactured enthusiasm or apologies.

The demo button is “Try this schedule.” The live action is “Approve these posts.” Neither should imply that connecting an account starts posting.
